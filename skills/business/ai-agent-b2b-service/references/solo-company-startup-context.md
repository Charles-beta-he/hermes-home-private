# Solo Company Startup Context (个人创业参考)

Applicability: solo developers or small teams considering registering a company to formalize AI/tech consulting services. Anti-condition: not legal advice; verify local regulations before acting.

## Common Startup Models

### Model 1: Open Source / Free Acquisition
- Strategy: Product free/open-source → user growth → monetize
- Best for: developer tools, infrastructure, PaaS/SaaS

### Model 2: B2B Funds B2C
- Strategy: B2B scenario validation + revenue → mature tech → B2C breakout
- Best for: hardware, AR/VR, robotics

### Model 3: KOL / Industry Penetration
- Strategy: Free for industry opinion leaders → upstream/downstream adoption → scale
- Best for: vertical SaaS, design tools

### Model 4: Technology Event Driven
- Strategy: Technical breakthrough → industry shock → organic acquisition
- Best for: frontier tech with measurable benchmarks

## Solo Company Registration Considerations

### General Advantages of Tech Hubs
- Mature ecosystem and policy support
- Talent pool for future collaboration
- Networking and partnership opportunities

### Registration Notes (verify local rules)
- Start as 个体工商户 (sole proprietorship) if sufficient
- Small-scale taxpayers: VAT thresholds vary by region
- Registration can wait until revenue is validated
- Consider: does registration location matter if work is remote?

### Recommended Path
1. Validate revenue first (personal capacity, 0-3 months)
2. Register company after stable income (3-6 months)
3. Productize repeatable work (6-12 months)
