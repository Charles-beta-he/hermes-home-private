# Domestic self-drive rental vehicle selection: long scenic route with 4 travelers

Applicability: mainland China flight-in self-drive trips where the user is choosing among rental SUVs for a long scenic itinerary (e.g. Xinjiang/Ili/Duku, grasslands, mountain roads), especially 4 travelers with multi-day luggage.

Anti-conditions: not for actual off-road/unsurfaced expedition routes, snow/ice specialist trips, or cases where the rental contract/route permission prohibits the intended roads. Do not reuse any session prices without rechecking live inventory.

## Core lesson
For long scenic self-drive with 4 people + luggage, the best vehicle is usually not the most “off-road looking” option. Prioritize **usable cabin + luggage space, long-distance comfort, route permission, insurance, rescue coverage, tire condition, and fuel practicality** over hard-core off-road image.

## Vehicle ranking pattern
1. **Mid-large / large family SUVs first** when budget is not the main constraint:
   - Examples: VW Talagon / Teramont / Tavendor class, Infiniti QX60, Exeed VX/Lanyue, other 6/7-seat mid-large SUVs.
   - Why: third row folded gives luggage space; rear seats are more comfortable; loading/unloading is easier; fatigue is lower on long transfer days.
2. **Spacious mainstream compact/mid-size SUVs next**:
   - Examples: Haval Dargo / H6, Changan CS75 Plus, Chery Tiggo 8, Jetour X70, Trumpchi GS4.
   - Good balance if mid-large SUVs are unavailable or insurance/vendor confidence is better.
3. **Hard-core boxy/off-road SUVs are conditional upgrades, not automatic first choice**:
   - Examples: Tank 300, BJ40, Deepal G318-like boxy SUVs.
   - Choose only when space is acceptable, tire/underbody/glass insurance and route permission are clear, and the user actually needs the added clearance/traction. Watch for higher fuel use and smaller/more awkward luggage space than large family SUVs.
4. **Avoid small SUVs/crossovers for 4-person long scenic trips**:
   - Examples: VW T-Roc/Tacqua/T-Cross class, Geely Binyue, Trumpchi GS3.
   - They may be cheap, but 4 people + luggage makes rear-seat comfort and trunk space a persistent pain point.

## Mandatory vendor checks before recommending a booking
Ask or verify:
- Can the vendor confirm the exact model, or is it only a “same group” booking? What are the possible substitutes?
- Fuel type: prefer gasoline/hybrid over pure EV for long remote mountain/grassland routes unless charging is explicitly planned.
- Can 4 people + 4 suitcases fit with the third row folded if 7-seat?
- Are intended regions/roads allowed (e.g. Ili, Nalati, Bayinbuluk, Duku Highway)?
- Unlimited mileage?
- Roadside rescue coverage and coverage geography.
- Insurance scope: tires, glass, underbody/chassis, deductible, non-deductible add-ons.
- Vehicle year/mileage, tire condition, spare tire/repair kit, warning triangle/reflective vest.
- Pickup/return buffers around flight anchors.
- If actual car condition is poor at pickup, can the vendor swap to same-or-larger class?

## Decision heuristic
If a user says “ignore budget, choose the most suitable car” for a 4-person scenic route, pick the **largest comfortable family SUV with clear insurance/permission** over a hard off-roader. The off-roader is a fallback when route/road condition needs it or the large SUV’s vendor/insurance is weak.
