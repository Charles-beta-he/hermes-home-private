# Hailar / Hulunbuir car-rental platform screening pattern — 2026-06 session

## When to use

Use when planning a Hulunbuir / Hailar self-drive trip where the traveler wants airport pickup and city / hotel return, especially when the route avoids night driving on grassland roads.

## Captured itinerary constraint

In this session the traveler had:

```text
6/16 evening: fly to Hohhot, overnight near airport
6/17 14:30: fly Hohhot -> Hailar
6/21 11:30: Hailar -> Hangzhou
```

The safer rental shape became:

```text
6/17 afternoon: Hailar airport pickup
6/20 evening: Hailar city hotel / city outlet return
6/21 morning: taxi to Hailar airport
```

This avoids forcing return inspection, outlet opening hours, and airport transfer into the morning of an 11:30 flight, and avoids night driving on grassland / border roads where cattle, sheep, poor lighting, crosswind, and fatigue are material hazards.

## Platform-screening lesson

Do not answer only at the abstract platform level. Large China car-rental platforms may support different pickup/return points, but the operational question is local inventory and outlet capability.

Verify four concrete facts:

1. Is Hailar Dongshan Airport available as an outlet, pickup point, or delivery point?
2. Is Hailar city available as a return outlet or hotel pickup / vehicle collection point?
3. Can the vendor meet the exact window: arrival-day airport handoff around 15:00-16:00 and penultimate-day city/hotel collection around 18:00-19:00?
4. What extra fees apply: airport delivery, hotel collection, cross-outlet return, overtime, or after-hours service?

## Evidence hierarchy for platforms

Prefer:

1. Direct-platform app / mini-program result for the exact city, dates, pickup, and return points.
2. Direct-platform phone confirmation with official customer service / outlet.
3. Aggregator listing with high order volume, high rating, clear supplier name, free-cancel rules, and written pickup/return terms.
4. Local private vendor only if contract, insurance, route permission, rescue, and deposit terms are explicit and auditable.

Do not treat SEO city pages as confirmation of current outlet inventory. In this session, public web pages showed broad city-entry / service mechanism signals, but did not reliably prove current Hailar airport + city return availability.

## Platform ranking pattern

For Hailar self-drive:

```text
Shenzhou / eHi official app or mini-program
> Ctrip high-rating/high-volume supplier
> Fliggy / Wukong high-rating supplier when vehicle type or local stock is better
> direct local shop only with strong written proof
```

Why:

- Direct platforms usually have cleaner contracts, standardized deposits, support, and fee categories.
- Aggregators are useful for local supply in tourist cities but require stricter supplier screening.
- Private vendors may be flexible but create more insurance, deposit, and rescue ambiguity.

## Recommended inquiry template

```text
你好，我计划 6月17日 14:30 到海拉尔东山机场，想 15:30 左右机场取车；
6月20日傍晚 18:00-19:00 回到海拉尔市区酒店，想酒店上门还车，或者市区门店还车。

路线大概是：
海拉尔机场 -> 额尔古纳 -> 恩和/室韦方向 -> 黑山头 -> 满洲里 -> 呼伦湖/新巴尔虎 -> 海拉尔市区。

5. 是否允许跑额尔古纳、黑山头、满洲里、呼伦湖、新巴尔虎？
6. 是否限里程？这条线总里程是否会超？
7. 是否油车？是否 SUV？具体车型和年份？
8. 保险包含哪些？有无不计免赔/补充险？
9. 爆胎、拖车、道路救援怎么处理？
10. 押金、违章押金、退还周期是多少？
11. 能否提前发实车视频、轮胎、里程表、行驶证/保险照片？
```

## Vehicle and safety default

For the Hailar -> Ergun -> Heishantou -> Manzhouli -> Hulun Lake / New Barag -> Hailar loop, prefer a gasoline SUV or at least a well-maintained gasoline sedan. Avoid making a pure EV the default unless charging points, range margin, weather, and route detours are confirmed.

If the vendor cannot confirm route permission, insurance, rescue, tire/breakdown handling, and pickup/return windows in writing, downgrade the route intensity or choose a stronger platform rather than forcing the cheapest listing.
