# Xinjiang Ili flight-in self-drive execution pattern (session-derived, 2026-06)

Applicability: Mainland China scenery-first self-drive trips where the user flies into a gateway city, rents a car/SUV, has must-go natural anchors, disallows night driving, and needs booking/packing/readiness decisions. This reference was derived from a Hangzhou ⇄ Urumqi, Ili + Sayram Lake + Nalati + Duku Highway plan for 4 people.

Anti-conditions:
- Do not reuse dates, hotel names, prices, or exact routing without rechecking live inventory, weather, road gates, and the user's current trip window.
- Do not treat city food/culture as irrelevant if the user explicitly asks for city exploration.
- Do not treat altitude/temperature bands as forecasts; use them as packing heuristics and recheck T-7/T-2/day-of.

## Planning corrections captured

1. **Scenery-first means cities are logistics anchors by day.**
   - If the user says the purpose is scenery, do not allocate a standalone city day for food or city street scenery.
   - Use cities for lodging, meals, refuel/supply, and evening walks only when night driving is disallowed.
   - Food should be embedded at meal points; specific restaurants come after hotel locations are fixed and native-map/app evidence is available.

2. **Night cannot be used for driving, but can be used locally.**
   - After arrival at the booked city/town, local dinner/night-snack/short nearby walk is acceptable.
   - Do not add remote night detours or far-away night markets before an early scenic day.

3. **Flight-in/out preserves scenic days; rail/soft sleeper must be scored by scenic-day loss.**
   - For short Xinjiang windows, public-transport alternatives may be comfortable but often consume 1–2 scenic days.
   - Compare transport by arrival threshold: can it preserve the first self-drive day? If not, state which scenic branch must be cut.

## Execution topology pattern

Use an execution-card output rather than a loose itinerary:
- D0 gateway flight arrival: sleep near airport/outbound route; no night pickup/long drive.
- D1 rental inspection + long transfer: conservative if flight/pickup is late; do not chase sunset when tired.
- Natural anchor days: morning/afternoon/meal/overnight blocks, each with fallback and do-not-do list.
- Branch days: define trigger conditions for scenic-upside branch vs stable branch.
- Final road-risk day: road gate + weather + driver-state + time gate before commitment.

For Ili-style routes, a useful branch structure is:
- Sayram Lake: big half-day, not automatically full day.
- Ili city: one dinner/night window, not a daytime city day unless requested.
- Zhaosu/Xiata: highest scenery priority; protect a strong weather window.
- Qiongkushitai vs Kalajun: scenic/lodging-upside branch vs stable timing branch.
- Nalati: precision duration based on self-drive ticket vs shuttle route.
- Bayinbuluk/Duku: only if timing, body state, and weather support it; otherwise keep lower-altitude lodging.

## Booking matrix pattern

Recommend booking now when the window is close and it is peak season, but prefer cancellable inventory and double-insurance on uncertain nights.

Priority order:
1. Scenic/lodging-bottleneck branch night (e.g., Qiongkushitai): two rooms, parking/transfer, dinner, hot water, heating/electric blanket/thick quilts, recent reviews, road-condition clarity.
2. Final road-risk landing night (e.g., post-Duku Dushanzi/Kuitun/Usu): cancellable, parking, late check-in, highway/return direction convenience, nearby hot food.
3. Pre-road-risk nights (e.g., Nalati/Bayinbuluk): cancellable and branchable; book both high-upside and stable fallback if cancellation terms allow.
4. Scenic support nights (e.g., Zhaosu/Tekes): use cancellable double-insurance when the previous day's depth/weather determines whether to push onward.
5. Ordinary gateway/city meal nights last, but still verify parking and late check-in.

Useful double-insurance pairs:
- After Xiata: Zhaosu vs Tekes.
- Grassland branch: Qiongkushitai vs Tekes/Xinyuan.
- Before Duku: Bayinbuluk vs Nalati/Xinyuan.

Cancel based on same-day or T-2 gates: weather, body state, road feedback, arrival before dark, and whether the scenic day ran deep or short.

## Altitude / temperature / clothing gate

For Xinjiang grassland + mountain-road routes, treat altitude, wind chill, and driver fatigue as first-class gates, even if the trip is not a high-altitude expedition.

Operational rule:
> If any traveler has persistent headache, nausea, unusual fatigue, shortness of breath, or a driver has slowed reactions/sleepiness, reduce scenic depth, avoid high-altitude overnight if possible, switch drivers or stop, and descend/return to a lower town if needed.

Typical July packing heuristic for Ili + Duku-style trips:
- City/low valley: summer clothes + light layer.
- High lake/grassland: windproof jacket, long pants, sun protection.
- Snow-mountain valley / Xiata: quick-dry base, fleece or light down, wind/rain shell, non-slip shoes, hot water/energy snacks.
- Bayinbuluk/high grassland overnight: fleece/light down + wind shell; confirm hotel hot water/heating/thick quilts.
- Duku high points: keep warm layers in the car cabin, not buried in luggage; short photo stops only under cold/wind/rain.

Group vehicle kit:
- Water, snacks/energy, tissues/wipes, trash bags, chargers, phone mount.
- Motion-sickness, GI, pain, cold/allergy meds, bandages, electrolyte packets.
- Optional 1–2 portable oxygen bottles as emergency comfort, not as permission to push through symptoms.

## T-7 / T-2 / day-of gate pattern

T-7: verify flight still preserves the first drive day, rental route permission/insurance/rescue, current road-opening trend, scenic-area rules, and broad weather trend.

T-2: choose branch lodging and scenic depth based on forecast and recent feedback: high lake wind/rain, mountain low cloud/rain, village-road mud risk, grassland highland cold, and Duku conditions.

Day-of: decide by hard gates:
- Long transfer: driver sleep + rental pickup time.
- Mountain/scenic area: opening, shuttle/last-entry, weather, queues, body state.
- Village/high-altitude lodging branch: can arrive before dark, dinner/check-in confirmed, road/weather/body state acceptable.
- Duku-style road: open, no temporary control, no severe rain/fog/rockfall risk, driver state good, enough time for safe descent/landing.

If a hard gate fails, downgrade immediately; do not preserve the itinerary shape at the expense of safety or return constraints.
